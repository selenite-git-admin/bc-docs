# Scoped lift of the D555 OC/CC-authoring hold for the finance/Odoo lane; metric activation stays certification-gated

## Context

The finance metric program's near-term blocker is the canonical layer (OC/CC over real Odoo), which the whole three-gate coverage program depends on. Keeping OC/CC frozen protects nothing the certification lane needs: certification gates metric ACTIVATION at M14/C8, strictly downstream of authoring, and that gate remains fully enforced under this lift — no metric reaches ACTIVE over Odoo without a certification record. Waiting for §11.A to finish before even building the canonical substrate serialises two independent tracks for no safety gain, while the substrate is the long-pole for first finance light. The scoped lift unblocks substrate construction and exemplar proof while the certification lane completes in parallel, and preserves one-then-many by requiring the journal_entry exemplar to prove E2E before any scaling. Recording the honest override framing (condition not met; scoped by decoupling) keeps the audit trail truthful rather than asserting a false condition-satisfied.

## Decision

Operator-authorized amendment to DEC-908548 (D555). LIFT the OC/CC-authoring hold for the finance/Odoo canonical lane, effective now. Observation Contracts, Canonical Contracts, and canonical_mapping over the admitted Odoo source (account.move first) MAY be authored and evaluated into Canonical Objects. STILL HELD: metric realization to ACTIVE over Odoo — a finance Metric Contract activates only through certification (PLN-457cd0 v12 §11.A / the C8 activation gate / D541 certification doctrine), which is unchanged by this lift. HONEST FRAMING: the literal D555 release condition ("certification-lane completion per §11.A") is NOT satisfied — §11.A is an active critical path (D532 consumer unbuilt = top blocker; certification wiring in progress via TSK-b2b12d; rules-before-judgement partial, currency template awaits review; grandfathered-34 an open operator fork). This is therefore a SCOPED DECOUPLING lift authorized by the operator, not a claim that the condition is met. Basis for decoupling: OC/CC authoring builds the canonical SUBSTRATE (Canonical Objects); it activates no metric, so it needs nothing from the certification lane, which gates activation strictly downstream. One-then-many is preserved: prove the journal_entry exemplar source→canonical E2E against pilot_ent before scaling to other entities. Access boundary from D555 is UNCHANGED: SC/AC/OC/CC schema authority = Odoo 19 vendor source (universal-mapping rule, never per-instance); the live pilot_ent Enterprise instance stays out of bounds for the authoring lane except coordinated windows established by the world-build arc. Not lifted: the independent mongo seed_metrics (TSK-6fd293) and db-bootstrap (TSK-8e49e0) holds — separate operator calls. Amends DEC-908548; does not supersede it (D524/D537/D555 remain decided).
