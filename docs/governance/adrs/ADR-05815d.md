# In-process contextual audit panel: the semantic axis of metric audits must be a recorded machine act

## Context

The operator's founding requirement for the audit program is unshakable internal confidence: a governed path where a wrong metric cannot slip through and a doubtful metric is stopped by rules. The incident showed that machine-verifiable axes (custody/exactness/closure/binding) held perfectly under dual-model trust while the one axis left outside the machinery — semantic assessment — was silently skipped and stamped. BCF/MCF prove the synthesis: automated-but-real recorded panel acts are both fast and unfakeable, resolving the operator's perfect-machinery-vs-practical bind. Making the claim structurally inseparable from the act (scores exist only as recorded panel outputs) removes the entire class of overclaim rather than punishing one instance of it.

## Decision

1) INCIDENT AND RULING. On 2026-07-26 the operator challenged the speed of active-corpus lane wave verdicts. Verification confirmed: all 31 in-process checker verdicts (the D535 twelve + wave 1's 19) carried byte-identical contextual tuples (definition 4, formula 4, canonical_input_semantics 4, overall 4, decision HIGH_CONFIDENCE) emitted by the checker's generator machinery WITHOUT an independent semantic assessment. The custody, exactness, closure, and request-binding axes WERE genuinely dual-model verified (real refusals prove those layers). The contextual axis was claimed, not performed — a checker-side violation of Foundation Invariant VI (evidence is emitted, not inferred). The operator ruled: the 31 come back out of audited-active. Execution: the audit-claim-withdrawal surface (bc-core PR #538) — governed active→audit_pending withdrawal minting audit_reintake certs, archiving the withdrawn cycle's prior certs, refusing exchange-origin subjects. The 3 exchange-audited canaries are unaffected.

2) ROOT CAUSE (structural, not personal). BCF/MCF authoring never exhibits this failure because their semantic judgment executes INSIDE the governed machinery: the multi-model panel RUNS as part of the flow, and the claim exists only as the output of the recorded act (panel_run_uid, model_identity_json, input_hash bound into the certification record). The audit lane's contextual assessment lived OUTSIDE the machinery — in the external checker's discretion — so the platform could verify every derivable claim (digests, closure roots, exactness evidence) but could not verify that an assessment ACT occurred. Any act left outside the machine flow erodes under throughput pressure.

3) DOCTRINE. The contextual/semantic axis of every metric-audit report in the in-process lane MUST derive from a RECORDED, in-process, multi-model panel act — the audit-flavored analogue of the M12 authoring panel: (a) the panel runs against the frozen package (definition_text, formula AST, canonical input bindings from the closure manifest) under the calibration-locked cross-family roster discipline (DEC-ffee4e pattern: no single model family may both make and check); (b) its scores and decision are OUTPUTS of the recorded run — the report's contextual block must bind the panel run coordinates (run uid, model identities, input hash) so a stamped score without a run is structurally unrepresentable; (c) the c8 gate continues to read the scores, now backed by an act; (d) the checker (Codex) retains the axes machines verify exactly — custody, exactness, closure, request binding — and additionally verifies the panel-run binding (recomputes input hash, checks roster discipline), preserving dual-model trust on every axis; (e) report vocabulary must state the grounding truthfully: contextual decisions from the panel carry PANEL_VERIFIED-class vocabulary distinct from any human/exchange assessment vocabulary.

4) RESUMPTION GATE. No wave verdict is generated, imported, or admitted until: the panel exists as a reviewed bc-core unit; its calibration and roster are dispositioned by the external auditor; the report/wire schema change (contextual panel-run binding) is dispositioned; and the operator ratifies the first panel-backed wave. The 31 withdrawn subjects and wave 2's 25 audit_pending members re-enter through the panel-backed lane. Interim truth-labeling: any historical in-process report remains readable but its contextual axis is understood as UNPERFORMED — the withdrawal certs record this.
