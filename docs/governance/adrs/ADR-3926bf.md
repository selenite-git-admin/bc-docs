# Kaveri Wave-1 invoice-chain unblock (re-scoped; JE-line premise withdrawn)

## Re-scope note

The original DEC-3926bf (extend the account.move.LINE / journal_entry_line observation+canonical projection A+C to carry the sub-ledger surface) was WRONG-TARGETED and is WITHDRAWN. Independent grain verification (Architect + Chief, mcf.metric_contract.grain_entity_id on bc_platform_dev, convergent) shows all 20 blocked Wave-1 AP/AR metrics are INVOICE-grained — 11 AP on Supplier Invoice (4471cb17), 9 AR on Customer Invoice (e3963e45) — NONE on journal_entry_line (07cef8c4). Only the DSO/DPO base measures (receivable_control_balance / payable_control_balance / receivable_billed_amount) are JE-line-grained, and those already work. So extending the JE-line projection unblocks none of these metrics; they read the invoice canonical. This ADR is re-scoped to the real repair at the invoice chains. The metrics sum GROSS amount (concept ae6e6528, present on Supplier Invoice) — not residual.

Grounded canonical state (bc_platform_dev): AP Supplier Invoice canonical cc-7174p v1.0.0 is ACTIVE (has gross_amount/due_date/posting_date; clearing_date ABSENT). AR Customer Invoice canonical cc-das36 has v1.0.0 superseded, v1.1.0 approved, v1.1.1 pending_provisioning — NONE active. clearing is a reconciliation property at account.move.LINE grain (full_reconcile_id → matched counter-line dates); the invoice (header) source has payment_state/amount_residual/invoice_date_due but no clearing-date column. No Supplier or Customer master business object exists (active BOs: cost_center, gl_account, journal_entry, journal_entry_line, product, supplier_invoice).

Foundation gate (DEC-79b62f) — PASS for the invoice-chain repair. (1) Why this location? The as_of metrics read the invoice canonical; the missing declaration is clearing_date on that canonical (a derived field, repair C/B) + the AR canonical's activation + a partner master BO (repair A/B) — all at the chain the metrics actually read. (2) Why not an upper layer? The invoice sources carry the reconciliation data; the gap is the canonical derivation/activation, not the source. (3) Why not a lower layer? A read/evaluator patch cannot synthesize a clearing_date never derived, nor activate a canonical, nor invent a master BO — forbidden lower-layer compensation. (4) Design or execution act? DESIGN act (DEC-c48b0f): missing declarations (clearing_date derivation; the master BO) named; the activation is a governed lifecycle act. Metric Contracts UNCHANGED — the new field resolves through binding (C); no MC/formula edit (MC-declares-semantic-inputs).

## Decision

D1 — clearing_date = OPTION A (derive on the invoice header chain). For the 12 as_of metrics' declared GROSS open-item model (binary open/settled), a single full-settlement clearing_date suffices, so derive clearing_date as a canonical field on the invoice canonicals (extend cc-7174p for AP; and the AR canonical once active) from the account.move.line reconciliation — NOT a re-source of the full residual/event schedule (Option B is overkill for a binary-open gross model). DECLARE the semantics precisely: clearing_date = the date of FULL reconciliation (residual→0 / full_reconcile), NULL while open; an invoice is OPEN at T iff clearing_date IS NULL OR clearing_date > T; source-agnostic ("fully reconciled", NOT the Odoo payment_state=paid flag). Folds DEC-93be49 (clearing_date = source-agnostic as_of-close semantic, resolved at the binding layer).

D2 — gross-vs-residual convention FLAG (Inv-I meaning check; route to Metric, do not silently accept, do not block D1). The as_of aging/overdue metrics sum GROSS (ae6e6528); the STANDARD aging ages OUTSTANDING (residual). ar_balance's gross is its DEC-fa7c63 D-4 identity (the open-item face value — settled). For the aging/overdue metrics, confirm the declared gross is the intended meaning; if residual aging is the standard intended, that is a governed MCV meaning-change (amount concept gross→residual) + Option B (residual-from-dated-events, DEC-fa7c63 D-2), NOT a silent switch. Absent such a decision, D1 (Option A, gross) realizes the declared meaning.

D3 — AR customer_invoice canonical activation (sequences FIRST, AR only). The 9 AR metrics cannot resolve until cc-das36 is ACTIVE. Activate it via the governed approved→active lifecycle (DEC-92f709 C8 door: deterministic chain-status green + provisioning complete; the pending_provisioning v1.1.1 or approved v1.1.0 → active; DEC-b36e15 pool-readiness). This is Metric's / the governed-flow's act (confirm the intended version + clear the provisioning/gate blocker), NOT a projection edit, and it SEQUENCES AHEAD of the AR clearing_date extension (activate the base canonical first; clearing_date is a later version on the active base). Confirm cc-das36 — NOT the document_number-grain cc-bmgcw/cc-net2q (L4 demand-pull) — is the canonical the e3963e45-grained AR metrics bind, to avoid activating the wrong one or a duplicate identity. Activation unblocks the 3 AR period_aggregate metrics (if their fields resolve); the 6 AR as_of need activation + clearing_date.

D4 — Supplier/Customer master-BO prerequisite (the 2 partner-keyed metrics). per_supplier_overdue_amount_0_30 (Supplier) and top_10_customer_ar_concentration (Customer) group by a partner reference, but no Supplier/Customer master BO exists. Onboarding a Supplier (+ Customer) master BO is a SEPARATE BCF business-object authoring prerequisite (SOP business-field-and-business-object-onboarding; repair A/B — a BO declaration), Foundation-gated when authored. partner_id on the invoice canonical is necessary but NOT sufficient. These 2 metrics DEFER behind the master-BO onboarding (the heaviest prerequisite); the other as_of metrics proceed on D1 + D3.

D5 — period_aggregate (8) + the quick-win. These need no clearing_date but each needs a per-metric binding-resolution pre-check on the active invoice canonical (count metrics may need workflow states not admitted — parent_state is uniformly 'posted'; payment-term / payment_state / write-off fields). posted_supplier_invoice_count is the clean near-term quick-win (Supplier Invoice grain, period_aggregate, cc-7174p active) after Metric's anchor+window re-bind (ruled clean separately) + a resolution check — independent of the clearing_date re-scope.

D6 — scope, MCs, delivery gating. MCs UNCHANGED (fields resolve through binding C). This is a governed contract act (canonical field derivation + the AR activation + a new master BO) authored through the SOPs, DRAFT/unactivated until re-reviewed + the operator decision grant. Delivery gating: (a) per the as_of realization boundary (TSK-9a2775 / TSK-c8af14 — the as_of gate must compute from the declared clearing_date / dated events, not a point-in-time snapshot); (b) the operator admission/activation grants; (c) explicit supersession where re-admission occurs (TSK-564901 world-stamp). No build, no activation, no run until re-review + the operator grant. The old #185 head (JE-line content) is superseded by this re-scope; grant #249 was void.
