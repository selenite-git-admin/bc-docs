# Kaveri Wave-1 invoice-chain unblock (re-scoped; JE-line premise withdrawn)

## Re-scope note

The original DEC-3926bf (extend the account.move.LINE / journal_entry_line observation+canonical projection A+C to carry the sub-ledger surface) was WRONG-TARGETED and is WITHDRAWN. Independent grain verification (Architect + Chief, mcf.metric_contract.grain_entity_id on bc_platform_dev, convergent) shows all 20 blocked Wave-1 AP/AR metrics are INVOICE-grained — 11 AP on Supplier Invoice (4471cb17), 9 AR on Customer Invoice (e3963e45) — NONE on journal_entry_line (07cef8c4). Only the DSO/DPO base measures (receivable_control_balance / payable_control_balance / receivable_billed_amount) are JE-line-grained, and those already work. So extending the JE-line projection unblocks none of these metrics; they read the invoice canonical. This ADR is re-scoped to the real repair at the invoice chains. ## Context

The metrics sum GROSS amount (concept ae6e6528, present on Supplier Invoice) — not residual.

Grounded canonical state (bc_platform_dev): AP Supplier Invoice canonical cc-7174p v1.0.0 is ACTIVE (has gross_amount/due_date/posting_date; clearing_date ABSENT). AR Customer Invoice canonical cc-das36 has v1.0.0 superseded, v1.1.0 approved, v1.1.1 pending_provisioning — NONE active. clearing is a reconciliation property at account.move.LINE grain (full_reconcile_id → matched counter-line dates); the invoice (header) source has payment_state/amount_residual/invoice_date_due but no clearing-date column. No Supplier or Customer master business object exists (active BOs: cost_center, gl_account, journal_entry, journal_entry_line, product, supplier_invoice).

Foundation gate (DEC-79b62f) — PASS for the invoice-chain repair. (1) Why this location? The as_of metrics read the invoice canonical; the missing declaration is clearing_date on that canonical (a derived field, repair C/B) + the AR canonical's activation + a partner master BO (repair A/B) — all at the chain the metrics actually read. (2) Why not an upper layer? The invoice sources carry the reconciliation data; the gap is the canonical derivation/activation, not the source. (3) Why not a lower layer? A read/evaluator patch cannot synthesize a clearing_date never derived, nor activate a canonical, nor invent a master BO — forbidden lower-layer compensation. (4) Design or execution act? DESIGN act (DEC-c48b0f): missing declarations (clearing_date derivation; the master BO) named; the activation is a governed lifecycle act. Metric Contracts UNCHANGED — the new field resolves through binding (C); no MC/formula edit (MC-declares-semantic-inputs).

## Decision

D1 — clearing_date = OPTION A (derive on the invoice header chain). For the 12 as_of metrics' declared GROSS open-item model (binary open/settled), a single full-settlement clearing_date suffices, so derive clearing_date as a canonical field on the invoice canonicals (extend cc-7174p for AP; and the AR canonical once active) from the account.move.line reconciliation — NOT a re-source of the full residual/event schedule (Option B is overkill for a binary-open gross model). DECLARE the semantics precisely: clearing_date = the date of FULL reconciliation (residual→0 / full_reconcile), NULL while open; an invoice is OPEN at T iff clearing_date IS NULL OR clearing_date > T; source-agnostic ("fully reconciled", NOT the Odoo payment_state=paid flag). Folds DEC-93be49 (clearing_date = source-agnostic as_of-close semantic, resolved at the binding layer).
ADMITTED INPUT PATH (required — the derivation needs reconciliation events the current OC does not map): declare the source/observation leg for the reconciliation data — account.partial.reconcile.max_date + the invoice line's full_reconcile_id (DEC-93be49) — the invoice join, and the aggregation (clearing_date = the max partial-reconcile date when the invoice is fully reconciled; NULL otherwise). The committed oc-h827j package maps NEITHER a reconciliation-event leg NOR full_reconcile_id, and the bc-core canonical resolver reads pinned active OC legs, so a NEW/extended observation leg for the reconciliation input is REQUIRED — an invoice-header canonical-field declaration ALONE cannot produce clearing_date. This collapses the earlier A/B framing: the field is DERIVED on the invoice canonical, but its INPUT is the admitted reconciliation leg joined to the invoice (the reconciliation data must be sourced, whichever way it is framed). Platform grounds the exact OC/source leg + join when authoring.

D2 — gross-vs-residual convention FLAG (Inv-I meaning check; route to Metric, do not silently accept, do not block D1). The as_of aging/overdue metrics sum GROSS (ae6e6528); the STANDARD aging ages OUTSTANDING (residual). ar_balance's gross is its DEC-fa7c63 D-4 identity (the open-item face value — settled). For the aging/overdue metrics, confirm the declared gross is the intended meaning; if residual aging is the standard intended, that is a governed MCV meaning-change (amount concept gross→residual) + Option B (residual-from-dated-events, DEC-fa7c63 D-2), NOT a silent switch. Absent such a decision, D1 (Option A, gross) realizes the declared meaning.

D3 — AR customer_invoice canonical activation (sequences FIRST, AR only). The 9 AR metrics cannot resolve until cc-das36 is ACTIVE. Activate it via the CANONICAL-CONTRACT activation lifecycle (approved→active for the CC: its deterministic chain-status green + provisioning complete; the pending_provisioning v1.1.1 or approved v1.1.0 → active; DEC-b36e15 pool-readiness). NB: this is the canonical-contract activation, NOT the DEC-92f709 C8 door, which governs metric-contract-VERSION (MCV) activation — a distinct gate. This is Metric's / the governed-flow's act (confirm the intended version + clear the provisioning/gate blocker), NOT a projection edit, and it SEQUENCES AHEAD of the AR clearing_date extension (activate the base canonical first; clearing_date is a later version on the active base). ⚠ MULTI-CC IDENTITY to resolve BEFORE activation (grounded): THREE canonical contracts bind business_object=customer_invoice at the SAME grain (key=name) — cc-das36 (the versioned primary: v1.0.0 superseded → v1.1.0 approved → v1.1.1 pending_provisioning) plus cc-bmgcw and cc-net2q (each v1.0.0 approved, described as "L4 demand-pull" variants; the "grain=document_number" is only in their description text, NOT the contract body, which is key=name). None is active. Activating one of three same-identity CCs risks a duplicate-active-per-identity (DEC-92f709 / the directory integrity bar). So the activation is gated on FIRST resolving the identity: pick the canonical one (likely cc-das36, the versioned primary) and supersede/archive the redundant cc-bmgcw/cc-net2q — Metric confirms which the e3963e45 AR metrics must bind + dispositions the others. Then activate. Activation unblocks the 3 AR period_aggregate metrics (if their fields resolve); the 6 AR as_of need activation + clearing_date.

D3b — AP header RE-ADMISSION is a HARD SHARED PREREQUISITE for ALL AP metrics (not inert cleanup). The admitted Supplier Invoice canonical facts are 100% PRE-DUMP earlier-world (Platform-verified), and the AP metrics read that header-sourced canonical — so even though cc-7174p is active, every AP metric (including the posted_supplier_invoice_count quick-win) is blocked by the contaminant until a clean v3_lc5 account.move HEADER re-admission replaces it. A plain re-admit/refresh cannot supersede (immutability refused the earlier refresh); the clean re-admission must SUPERSEDE the pre-dump headers by EXPLICIT REFERENCE — the world/dump stamp + current-world selector of TSK-564901 (Inv-III/IV). So TSK-564901 (now elevated from parked) is a hard dependency of the AP arc, and its supersession mechanism must be coordinated with Platform's header-re-admission manifest. posted_supplier_invoice_count is the near-term AP quick-win only AFTER this clean header re-admission (+ its anchor+window re-bind + a resolution check).
AR TOO (Codex round-2): the AR Customer Invoice canonical also routes through account.move. Unless Demo/Platform ESTABLISH that AR has a separate clean current-world source, the same clean-world header re-admission + explicit world-stamped supersession (TSK-564901) is a prerequisite BEFORE AR evaluation as well. So treat the clean-world gate as applying to BOTH AP and AR (every invoice-grained metric sourced from account.move) until an AR clean-source is proven; confirm AR's account.move source contaminant status with Demo.

D4 — Supplier/Customer master-BO prerequisite (the 2 partner-keyed metrics). per_supplier_overdue_amount_0_30 (Supplier) and top_10_customer_ar_concentration (Customer) group by a partner reference, but no Supplier/Customer master BO exists. Onboarding a Supplier (+ Customer) master BO is a SEPARATE BCF business-object authoring prerequisite (SOP business-field-and-business-object-onboarding; repair A/B — a BO declaration), Foundation-gated when authored. partner_id on the invoice canonical is necessary but NOT sufficient. These 2 metrics DEFER behind the master-BO onboarding (the heaviest prerequisite); the other as_of metrics proceed on D1 + D3.

D5 — period_aggregate (8) + the quick-win. These need no clearing_date but each needs a per-metric binding-resolution pre-check on the active invoice canonical (count metrics may need workflow states not admitted — parent_state is uniformly 'posted'; payment-term / payment_state / write-off fields). posted_supplier_invoice_count is the near-term AP quick-win (Supplier Invoice grain, period_aggregate, cc-7174p active, no clearing_date) — but now GATED on the D3b clean v3_lc5 header re-admission (the pre-dump contaminant is a hard shared AP prerequisite), in addition to Metric's anchor+window re-bind (ruled clean separately) + a resolution check. It is independent of the clearing_date work, not of the header re-admission.

D6 — scope, MCs, delivery gating. MCs UNCHANGED (fields resolve through binding C). This is a governed contract act (canonical field derivation + the AR activation + a new master BO) authored through the SOPs, DRAFT/unactivated until re-reviewed + the operator decision grant. Delivery gating: (a) per the as_of realization boundary (TSK-9a2775 / TSK-c8af14 — the as_of gate must compute from the declared clearing_date / dated events, not a point-in-time snapshot); (b) the operator admission/activation grants; (c) explicit supersession where re-admission occurs (TSK-564901 world-stamp). No build, no activation, no run until re-review + the operator grant. The old #185 head (JE-line content) is superseded by this re-scope; grant #249 was void.
