# Extend observed-never-converted currency to the GL / journal-entry-line chain (cc-x13pb) so the per-LE DSO evaluates through the governed evaluator — amends DEC-6e951e

## Context

DEC-6e951e (decided 2026-10-20) already resolves the multi-currency fork — BareCount never converts (DEC-7bccf6); option B plus the source's own booked local-currency amount — and already decides a fail-closed currency gate at runtime, lifecycle/certification/activation, read-model freshness and authoring (its D4 a–d). So there is NO fresh A-vs-B-vs-C decision to make; re-deciding it would re-open locked doctrine. The genuine residual, surfaced during the per-LE DSO prove-one (TSK-17a8bf), is that DEC-6e951e scoped its observe (D1) and project (D2) acts to the Customer-Invoice chain (oc-3kgrr / cc-das36). The General-Ledger chain that actually carries the DSO family — journal_entry_line, grain CC cc-x13pb — projects no currency field in either 2.0.0 or 3.0.0, so MetricEvaluationOrchestrator fails closed with MC_DEFECT_LOCAL_CURRENCY_MISSING for the DSO and its amount upstreams (both declared aggregation_currency_code='local_currency'). The per-LE DSO is therefore only provable today by SQL recompute, never through BareCount's own governed mechanism — the real blocker to a governed-evaluator Validated-vs-LC5 verdict. This amendment extends DEC-6e951e's observe→project pattern to the GL chain. DESIGN ONLY; it authorizes no build, OC/CC authoring, activation or live act. Grounded 2026-10-24 at bc-docs origin/main c327c94; governing authority DEC-ffbc24/D673.

## Decision

This **amends DEC-6e951e** (decided 2026-10-20) and does **not** supersede it. DEC-6e951e resolves the currency fork (no BareCount FX; option B + source-booked local currency) and decides a fail-closed currency gate at runtime, lifecycle, read-model and authoring (its D4 a–d), but scopes its projection acts (D1 observe, D2 project) to the Customer-Invoice chain (oc-3kgrr / cc-das36). The General-Ledger chain that carries the DSO family — `journal_entry_line`, grain CC `cc-x13pb` — projects no currency field in 2.0.0 or 3.0.0, so the governed evaluator fails closed with `MC_DEFECT_LOCAL_CURRENCY_MISSING` for `receivable_days_sales_outstanding` and its amount upstreams (`receivable_billed_amount`, `receivable_control_balance`, both `aggregation_currency_code='local_currency'`). This amendment extends DEC-6e951e's project→gate pattern to the GL chain so the DSO family evaluates governed and can be Validated-vs-LC5.

**Material difference from the Customer-Invoice chain — the extension is NOT a mechanical mirror (grounded 2026-10-25: Act C package `barecount-devhub:artifacts/metric-audit/validate-kaveri-oct21/PACKAGE-actC-jel-cc-local-currency-projection-dso-2026-10-25.md`; OC `d702580d@1.1.0` `so_schema`; `fact.so_sc_snedd`; bc-core `main`).** The journal-entry-line fact carries **no functional-currency field**: `company_currency_id` is absent from the OC `so_schema` and NULL in the admitted facts; `currency_id` is the **transaction** currency (unmapped); the functional **amount** is already observed as `balance` (mapped to `posted_amount`; Odoo books `balance` in company currency). So on this chain the functional currency is **LE-DERIVED**, not observed, and D1/D2 below replace DEC-6e951e D1's observe-`company_currency_id` mechanism for this chain. This refinement folds the Chief-routed Act C sourcing decision and corrects a field-name defect in that package (`currency_code` → `local_currency_code`).

**D1 (GL). The functional currency is LE-DERIVED, not observed (repair C — governed reference derivation).** On the journal-entry-line chain the local/functional currency is NOT a per-row observation. The fact carries no functional-currency field (grounded: OC `d702580d@1.1.0` `so_schema` has no `company_currency_id`; `currency_id` is the transaction currency and is unmapped), while the functional **amount** is already observed as `balance` (→ `posted_amount`). The functional currency of a GL line is, by definition, the currency of its company / legal entity — a single-source-of-truth attribute of the resolved LE, not a datum to re-observe per row. So the local currency is derived from the §2-resolved `legal_entity_id` (the same universal DEC-ffbc24 §2 selector that stamps `legal_entity_id` on the CO) → `dim_legal_entity.currency_code` (the DEC-4aa1ff D10 attribute). **No `res.currency` observation leg and no new OC source field are needed for the functional currency, and there is NO FX** (DEC-6e951e): `balance` is already source-booked in the LE currency; the projection only TAGS it with the LE's code. This **replaces DEC-6e951e D1's observe-`company_currency_id` mechanism for this chain** — that mechanism is correct for the Customer-Invoice chain (where `account.move` exposes both document and company currency), but the GL line does not expose a functional currency. `currency_id` (transaction currency) is explicitly NOT the local currency and is not projected for the `local_currency` policy.

**D2 (GL). Project the LE-derived `local_currency_code` on cc-x13pb (repair B).** A new major `cc-x13pb` version (4.0.0; key-form `canonical_id_v1` preserved, all current fields carried verbatim) projects exactly one new canonical field — **`local_currency_code`** — the LE-derived code of D1. **Field-name correction (grounded on bc-core `main`):** the field MUST be named `local_currency_code`, the exact field the `local_currency` policy resolves — `metric-spec-assembler.ts` does `resolveField(ccFields, 'local_currency_code')`, and `currency-policy-support.ts` states "local_currency keys on `local_currency_code` (not `currency_code`)". The Act C package's draft successor projected `currency_code`; that is the DOCUMENT-currency field (consumed only by the `document_currency` policy) and would leave the gate still failing — it must be corrected to `local_currency_code`. The field binds to a new local-currency-code BCF concept (prerequisite 1 below; `representation_term: code`), and a `resolution_rules` `assert_equal` on `local_currency_code` records it. `local_currency_code` equals the row's resolved LE functional currency by construction (it is derived from that LE); the `local_currency_mismatch` refusal still guards a row whose resolved LE cannot supply a functional currency. **As-of caveat (Codex gen-e5fc38-01):** the DEC-4aa1ff D10 posting-date-as-of resolution is NOT yet implemented — `local-currency-cross-check.ts` uses the LE's **current** functional currency, as-of pending `TSK-739801`. The interim single-LE / time-invariant proof carries the **current-not-as-of** evidence label and **refuses** a historically changed functional currency; the as-of form is an execution dependency on TSK-739801, not presumed here. Local-currency aggregation never spans LEs (DEC-4aa1ff D5).

**D3. Policy of the DSO family (the semantic design act).** The two amount upstreams KEEP `aggregation_currency_code='local_currency'`: they aggregate the source's own booked company-currency amount — no FX (DEC-7bccf6). The DSO ratio itself stays `not_applicable` (a days figure, not a currency amount). The alternative — flipping the upstreams to `document_currency` or `single_currency_required` — is **REJECTED**: it changes the metric's declared meaning (a per-currency or single-currency receivable balance is not the DSO family's meaning) and is lower-layer compensation for the missing projection. The functional amount is the already-observed `balance` (→ `posted_amount`), source-booked in the LE currency (no new amount field, no FX). GL-line sign semantics (debit/credit → signed `balance`) are a declared meaning, proven against source rows through the LC5 seam before any re-author, not assumed (mirror DEC-6e951e).

**D4. The fail-closed gate is unchanged and already decided.** DEC-6e951e D4 (runtime refuses; lifecycle/cert/activation refuse; read-model freshness; authoring refuses) governs this chain too. This amendment adds **no** new gate: it supplies the CC projection the existing gate **requires**. Supplying the projection **targets** moving the GL chain off `MC_DEFECT_LOCAL_CURRENCY_MISSING` toward green — it is not, by itself or by this proposed ADR, a green/Validated-vs-LC5 verdict. The execution unit must still prove, before any green or Validated-vs-LC5 claim: a resolved `legal_entity_id` on every selected row; `local_currency_code` present and single-valued per row (= the resolved LE's functional currency, by derivation); the source-booked signed amount (`balance` → `posted_amount`) and its sign; and the evaluator + lifecycle gates passing. No amber-advisory path is introduced.

## Invariants engaged

- **I — meaning produced once at its boundary:** the functional amount is observed once (`balance`) at the observation boundary; the LE's functional currency is resolved once by the governed §2 selector (DEC-ffbc24) — neither is converted or re-derived at a lower layer.
- **IV — references are explicit:** `local_currency_code` is a reference-derived field from the version-pinned resolved LE (`dim_legal_entity`, DEC-4aa1ff D10), not an implicit or head-resolved value.
- **VI — evidence emitted, not inferred:** no FX; the source's own booked company-currency figure (`balance`) is authoritative, and the currency code is the governed LE attribute, not an inferred label.

Consistent with DEC-7bccf6 and DEC-6e951e.

## Foundation gate (DEC-79b62f)

1. **Why location B (CC projection) sourced from a C binding — and NO A observation?** The local currency is LE-derived: it is projected on the CC (B) from the governed §2 LE resolution (C — the `legal_entity_id` binding), reading `dim_legal_entity.currency_code`. There is **no** observation (A) step for the functional currency: the GL fact does not carry it, and the functional amount (`balance`) is already observed. This removes the A-precondition the earlier DEC-6e951e-mirror draft assumed.
2. **Why not an upper layer alone?** The currency semantics (observe-never-convert; the functional currency is the LE's attribute) are already fully specified by DEC-6e951e / DEC-7bccf6 / DEC-4aa1ff. This is their correct application to a second chain whose fact shape differs, not a new semantic.
3. **Why not a lower layer?** Flipping the upstreams to `not_applicable`/`single_currency_required`, summing blind, or projecting the transaction `currency_id`, is lower-layer compensation that changes metric meaning — refused (D1, D3).
4. **Design act or execution act (DEC-c48b0f)?** The general obligation (any currency-denominated MC's grain CC must project the field its policy needs) is already decided (DEC-6e951e D4(d)). The DESIGN ACTs this refinement names are: (i) the sourcing — the GL functional currency is **LE-derived, not observed** (D1); (ii) the semantic policy — the DSO receivable upstreams keep `local_currency` (not document/single) over the source-booked `balance`; and (iii) the field-name correction — the projected field is **`local_currency_code`**, not `currency_code` (D2). Each is a declaration, not a detector.

## Scope and status

Scope: the GL / `journal_entry_line` chain (`cc-x13pb`) and the DSO family (`receivable_days_sales_outstanding` + its two amount upstreams). It folds the Chief-routed Act C sourcing decision (LE-derived functional currency) and the `currency_code`→`local_currency_code` field-name correction. It does **not** re-open DEC-6e951e's decided fork or gate, and authorizes no build, OC/CC authoring, activation or live act — each keeps its own SOP rail + review + operator gate. **Status: proposed**; it is additive (amends, not supersedes); DEC-6e951e stays decided. Ratification is the operator's recorded grant.
