# Retire the signed audit courier; preserve dual-model maker/checker trust and the admission gate at full strength

## Context

Three metrics admitted after weeks; zero numeric defects found in any metric. Every defect chased was in the apparatus proving an audit happened, never in the audit's subject. Three mechanisms drove the non-termination: both engines under construction simultaneously so no proof stayed valid (two green rehearsals went stale within hours of being issued); append-only plus one-shot identity meaning failures spawned recovery institutions (epoch cuts, supersession classes, un-strand sweeps) rather than retries; and a governance layer that became its own defect surface (errata correcting ADRs, memos correcting memos, halts over artifact custody location, and finally a database mutated on a chat-inferred authorization). The doctrine of record (D519/D523) already states this engine provides GOVERNANCE CONFIDENCE, not certification — the fidelity being paid for exceeded what the doctrine asks. The operator's requirement is that a wrong metric cannot reach active and a doubtful one is stopped until a rule addresses it: that is a property of the gate and the CRV rule catalog, both of which are preserved unchanged, and neither of which depends on transport. The dual-model maker/checker property — uncorrelated failure between model families — is likewise independent of transport and is preserved explicitly. Evidence the maker/checker mechanism earns its keep: in one session the checker caught the platform's CF-R3 recompute defect and rehearsal pin-shape defect, while the platform caught the checker's r3 authority inheritance, packet-shape regressions and improper authorization inference; neither party would have caught its own.

## Decision

Operator-ratified 2026-07-25. The metric-audit program keeps its evaluation substance and its dual-model trust property, and retires the cryptographic two-party transport that has consumed the program's cost.

WHAT IS RETIRED (the courier, dev corpus): KMS envelope signing of audit report/decision packets; append-only response-feed sequence chains with prior-digest continuity; epoch cuts and successor feed registration as a recovery mechanism; one-shot request/run/packet identity as the delivery model; cross-database outbox/pickup transport; auditor bootstrap-authority pinning as a gate on platform progress; cross-repo custody relay of packet artifacts. Rationale: this machinery has never once stopped an incorrect metric. Every time it fired it produced a false negative on a correct metric (KMS alias, r2 hardcode, epoch cut #1, pickup wall, r3 authority stamp on ten correct verdicts, closure-grounding halt, bootstrap head mismatch), and each such event cost an epoch cut, burned request identities, or stranded subjects.

WHAT IS PRESERVED AT FULL STRENGTH (the gate — this is the operator's stated requirement of unshakable internal confidence): the DB-enforced c1-c12 intrinsic decision refusal gate; admission recomputation byte-comparing the recomputed package signature and closure against the frozen snapshot; exactness and reproducibility evidence hash-bound to frozen package bytes (three labelled bases per DEC-414ba2); the wire coherence validators (V-D1, V-D3, V-D8, V-D9, V-R8, CF-R3, CF-R4); DB triggers that make invalid states unrepresentable rather than merely refused; halt-on-refusal; the rehearsal lane (DEC-deb4d4) as the pre-commit conformance surface; and the auditor's 22-rule contextual-reference catalog (CRV-001..CRV-022) in full — verified 2026-07-25 to contain zero references to feeds, signatures, KMS, outbox or pickups.

DUAL-MODEL MAKER/CHECKER TRUST IS THE LOAD-BEARING PROPERTY (operator's stated reason for the second engine) and is preserved by four non-negotiable invariants: (1) the checker is always a DIFFERENT MODEL FAMILY from the maker — a Claude-authored checker destroys the property entirely; roster changes require operator sign-off, following the DEC-ffee4e calibration-lock pattern. (2) INDEPENDENT RE-DERIVATION — the checker derives from substrate, never from the maker's intermediate work; grounding on maker-exported bytes is a recorded fallback, never the default. (3) REFUSAL STICKS — the checker can stop an activation and the maker cannot override it; only rules clear it. (4) THE MAKER CANNOT AUTHOR THE CHECKER'S VERDICT — verdict artifacts are committed in the checker's own repository under its own history and imported citing that digest; git custody replaces cryptographic custody for the dev corpus.

TWO CLAUSE RE-SPECIFICATIONS (out of ~34 checks; everything else untouched): CRV-002 re-binds from 'published request' to 'retained request artifact + digest' (auditor rule change — requires Codex disposition). Platform gate check c11 re-specifies from 'feed registration + signer + checkpoint continuity' to 'decision cites independent-origin checker evidence' (platform change — own PR, own review, DB work under DBCP).

HONESTY REQUIREMENT (non-negotiable): the record must permanently distinguish exchange-audited from in-process-evaluated provenance via a distinct basis label visible in the decision and the certification record. This program exists because 'active' was a state flag with no evidence behind it; producing 'active' under a lighter path that is indistinguishable from the three fully-exchanged canaries would recreate that defect in a more sophisticated form. Canary A (paid_customer_invoice_count), B-prime (total_customer_receipts) and canary C (05956df4) stand as the completed, three-times-proven demonstration of the full signed exchange — that proof is the deliverable of the exchange arc and is now closed.

OPERATING CHANGES: freeze the checker pipeline (no mid-flight fixes; findings park to a v2 list — moving targets invalidated two green rehearsals in one day); batch the ceremony (one authorization per wave, not per member and per PR); recover the twelve lane-stranded subjects through the simplified path rather than further epoch machinery.

REDIRECTED PRIORITY: the two largest remaining threats to internal confidence are NOT transport — they are (a) the ~180 avg-family metrics with no proof path under either prover (doctrine question to the checker, open for weeks) and (b) the ~340 legacy actives still carrying a state flag with no evidence. The 22 CRV rules — grain, units, temporal and aggregation semantics, population boundary, formula-AST binding, canonical allowlist resolution, source-literal leakage, reference sufficiency, currency and sign convention, edge-behavior disposition, taxonomy alignment — have been applied to only three metrics to date. Retiring the courier is what allows the rule audit to actually run at corpus scale.

RESURRECTION CONDITION: if a real external audit engagement arises (customer- or regulator-facing certification rather than internal confidence), the exchange is rebuilt as its own scoped project. It is proven, documented, and the rehearsal lane and parity vector sets survive intact for that purpose.
