# AC/OC declare semantic preconditions in the contract — the Scanner verifies declarations, never invents them

## Context

Operator observation (2026-08-04, ratified as direction 2026-08-05): after seeing how many implicit anchors Odoo carries, a Connector-as-driver is insufficient to onboard a source system. The evidence is first-hand: four semantic preconditions were paid for in the pilot at a day of diagnosis each, and none is detectable by connectivity or field-presence checks — the field is there, it just does not mean what the contract assumes. The substrate confirms the gap is real: contract.observation_field_map carries source_field_name/is_required/transform_hint (field presence has a declaration) while NO surface declares the conditions under which those fields mean anything. Declaring preconditions in the contract keeps meaning at its boundary (Invariant I), gives the future Scanner a governed source of truth instead of a hardcoded checklist, and honours the D541 lesson (the currency-partition gap was a design-plane omission; an execution-plane check was built first and mistaken for the remedy — this time the declaration comes first by construction).

## Decision

The Admission Contract and Observation Contract grammars gain a declaration surface for SEMANTIC PRECONDITIONS on the source system: conditions under which the contract's field meanings hold at all.

1. DECLARATION SHAPE (grammar-level; concrete storage lands with the implementing DBCP): semantic_preconditions[] on the AC/OC version, each entry = { precondition_code, subject_kind (system_config | module_presence | field_semantics | scoping), subject_ref (e.g. res.company.anglo_saxon_accounting, product.category.property_cost_method, module:mrp, company_dependent:standard_price), expected_value, rationale_text, on_violation (block | warn) }. Declarations are versioned WITH the contract, immutable per version, part of the contract's meaning (Invariant I: meaning is evaluated once — a precondition is part of what a field MEANS).

2. THE SEED LIST is the four preconditions measured first-hand in the Odoo pilot (2026-08-04), every one of which passes a connector test and a field-presence test while silently changing what a field means: (a) anglo_saxon_accounting off -> the COGS field exists and stays empty; (b) cost method standard vs average -> standard_price denotes a different quantity; (c) company-dependent fields -> the same field read under another company returns another value (three separate production defects); (d) module not installed -> the concept does not exist at all.

3. THE SCANNER (tenant-level coverage/compatibility tool, future) is an EXECUTION-PLANE VERIFIER that READS declarations from admitted AC/OC versions and evaluates them against a tenant's connected instance. It never invents checks: no declaration, no scan finding. This is the D541 plane discipline applied in advance — the declaration (design plane) exists before any detector (execution plane), so the detector can never be mistaken for the remedy. Scanner output is coverage/compatibility diagnostics (repair-location F), never a gate that mutates contracts.

4. THREE CONCERNS STAY SEPARATE: connectivity (can the record be read — Connector), coverage (is the function represented — module/table presence), compatibility (does the field mean what the contract assumes — semantic preconditions). Only the third is new, and it is the one a Connector-as-driver can never answer.

5. Foundation touch: the-contract-grammar.md AC and OC artifact definitions are amended to name the declaration surface with this ADR as authority. TSK-f47002 moves from parked to planned as the implementation carrier.
