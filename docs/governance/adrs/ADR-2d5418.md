# Hardened meta-schema change control: batch-atomic CAS approvals, principal-bound review, DB-frozen terminal evidence, agent-author identity

## Context

The meta-schema rows in `contract.contract_meta_schema` are the DB residence of the
contract grammar (founder rule 2026-05-09: foundational assets in DB, locked, editable
only with approval). The original TSK-d57036 approval path was found unfit during the
2026-08-15 OC/AC grammar sync review: its approve was explicitly non-atomic ("atomic-ish"),
coupled changes were a client-side loop, no baseline/CAS or concurrency control existed,
reviewer identity was caller-supplied display text, and cache invalidation was
process-local (external-audit findings M1–M5, exchange 23f55aa9). Successive reviews
added F1 (terminal review evidence rewriteable), F2 ("merged-dormant" was
deployment-unsafe), and F3 (a terminal row could be fabricated by direct INSERT).
Separately, principal-bound self-approval refusal made a second identity operationally
necessary: with a single human principal, the author of a change request could never be
distinct from its approver.

## Decision

contract.contract_meta_schema is editable ONLY via the hardened MetaSchemaChangeRequestService (PR #693 head aa116665, merged ce052ccf; migration 61 applied 2026-08-15): requests capture an immutable baseline (JSON + canonical schemaDigest) and the authenticated author principal at creation; coupled changes form an explicit batch (shared batch_uid); approval is ONE database transaction across all schema writes and terminal transitions with FOR-UPDATE locks, baseline CAS (stale refuses, never overwrites), pre-compilation, and exactly-one-affected-row proofs; reviewer identity is the authenticated principal with self-approval refused and changeRecordUid + auditorResponseRef REQUIRED; the DB governance guard (BEFORE INSERT OR UPDATE, migration 61) enforces born-pending insertion, captured-fact immutability, full terminal-row freeze, and evidence-carrying transitions; validator caches are digest-keyed (coherent by construction across processes, no invalidation broadcast); the governed routes fail closed with an explicit rollout contract until the migration-61 substrate exists.

Author/reviewer separation is operationalized by the bc-ai-service agent-author Cognito identity (sub 03bb82a5): the assistant authors under the service principal (devhub_get_cognito_token user:'service'; BC_SERVICE_* credentials operator-provisioned in bc-core/.env, never assistant-held), the operator approves under their own principal.

Driven by external-audit findings M1-M5 (exchange 23f55aa9), F1/F2 (dfed4c5e), F3 (812aa950); accepted 342193d2; m61 DBCP accepted 210e1567, applied with closure 8a3d774b. First governed use: the OC/AC pair+source_filter grammar sync (batch 00c48cbf, acceptance ae250de5, operator-approved, closure e67e40c0). Sixteen adversarial clone vectors CI-executed at the accepted head. Supersedes the TSK-d57036 best-effort approve path.
