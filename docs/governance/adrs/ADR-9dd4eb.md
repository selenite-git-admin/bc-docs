The operator formally accepts and announces closure of the Platform DB Foundation program (TSK-cc348a / DEC-c40e7a), issued directly to this session on 2026-09-21 ("formally accept closure"). 2. W2 — DISPOSED closed-at-substance, as a governed decision (this DEC), superseding the prior session-message-only basis (d597 MSG-443). The W2 productization gates (D5 BYO-DB, D9 escrow, D10 recovery objectives, D11 residency — D11 legal-gated) are deferred to a deliberate go-live decision; the tenant/W2 convergence dependency (MLS-20, per DEC-568d0b) is a dependency, not a block.
