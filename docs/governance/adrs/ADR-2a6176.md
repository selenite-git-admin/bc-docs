# DSO numerator trade-scope realized by a receivable_subtype account-classification facet (DEC-c46354 classify_by_binding D6; realizes DEC-fa7c63 D-1)

## Context

DEC-fa7c63 D-1 (the decided DSO family meaning, 2026-09-28) already scopes the DSO numerator population to posted lines on E's CUSTOMER receivable-control accounts (trade receivables). The Tax Current Account-Receivable is not a customer receivable-control account, so it was never in the decided population; D-9(a) classifies a non-reconciling receivable line as a realization defect, "reported as a refusal, never bridged." Today the numerator (receivable_control_balance) binds to account_type_code='receivable', which is atomic over a 17-value closed set and therefore admits the tax-receivable account and non-customer general-journal lines — the ~0.35% over-count the LC5 referee surfaced (Tax Current A/R + 261 GJ lines). This is a realization defect against the already-decided trade scope, not a new meaning.

Metric ran both substrate checks on the JE-Line grain (read-only): account_class_code = {asset, liability, equity, revenue, expense} does not separate tax-AR from trade-AR (both 'asset'); there is no customer-account-identifier concept selected onto the JE-Line grain; the only account distinguishers on the grain are account_type_code (atomic), ledger_account_identifier (a raw account id), and document_type_code (the wrong axis for a balance). So no existing declared concept isolates customer trade receivable-control accounts. The three forbidden shortcuts: a raw ledger-account-id filter (lower-layer compensation); and a document_category filter (which drops settling payments from the cumulative-to-date stock and collapses the numerator toward the billed amount).

A governed account-classification mechanism already exists: DEC-c46354 (implemented) declares classify_by_binding with tenant.gl_account_classification_binding and contract.gl_account_classification_default, output_facet in {account_class, account_type, cash_flow_category}, seeded from platform defaults and confirmed at source-onboarding (the FSV classification review), provenance-stamped and fail-closed for unseeded tenants. Its D6 states the mechanism generalizes to new classification facets, each ADR-governed, never ad hoc.

**Three tests any trade-scope basis must pass** (the Architect's ruling MSG-495046): T1 — preserve the net cumulative-to-date balance (keep the settling payment/clearing postings); T2 — a DECLARED concept, not a raw ledger-account-id; T3 — cleanly exclude tax-AR and non-customer GJ.

**Foundation self-check (DEC-79b62f).** (1) *Why this location?* The enabling declaration is A/B — the account-classification facet and its governed closed vocabulary, declared through DEC-c46354's binding; the numerator fix is C, binding to the facet. (2) *Why not an upper layer?* The meaning is not underspecified — DEC-fa7c63 D-1 already scopes to customer trade receivable-control accounts; this realizes it, it does not re-decide it. (3) *Why not a lower layer?* A metric-level raw-account-id filter or a document_category filter is lower-layer compensation for a missing declaration; the correct fix declares the classification, and the over-count is a D-9(a) realization defect repaired at the classification, never bridged. (4) *Design or execution act?* A design act (DEC-c48b0f): a missing declaration — the account-classification facet isolating customer trade receivable-control accounts — is named and declared; the per-tenant account-to-subtype mapping is execution at source-onboarding. The Chief co-signs; the operator gave GO on the path (2026-10-14, via the Chief).

## Decision

D1 — Declare a new account-classification facet receivable_subtype under the existing DEC-c46354 gl_account_classification binding_kind (its D6 new-facet path). Governed closed value set: trade, tax_statutory, other_non_trade (the D430 value-set gate applies). trade = a customer trade receivable-control account; tax_statutory = a tax or statutory receivable account (e.g. Tax Current A/R); other_non_trade = any other receivable-natured account that is not a customer trade receivable-control account. The facet classifies the ACCOUNT; the JE-Line carries it by reference to its account, exactly as account_type_code is carried. Meaning is declared once (Invariant I) and referenced explicitly (Invariant IV).

D2 — The canonical field that carries the receivable subtype declares classify_by_binding with params.output_facet='receivable_subtype' and binding_kind='gl_account_classification'. The CC declares THAT the field is binding-classified and INTO which closed vocabulary; it carries no tenant mapping content — platform contracts stay universal (DEC-c46354 D1).

D3 — The per-tenant account-to-subtype mapping lives in tenant.gl_account_classification_binding (DEC-c46354 D2), seeded from a new receivable_subtype default set in contract.gl_account_classification_default (DEC-c46354 D3), confirmed and overridable at source-onboarding via the FSV classification review (DEC-c46354 D4/D5, as amended to the source-onboarding stage). Provenance-stamped; fail-closed for an unseeded tenant (DEC-c46354 D4b) — a missing receivable_subtype is a refusal, never an inferred value (Invariant VI).

D4 — The DSO numerator (receivable_control_balance) re-binds (repair location C) its receivable-population predicate to: account_type_code='receivable' AND receivable_subtype='trade'. This realizes DEC-fa7c63 D-1's "customer receivable-control accounts (trade receivables)." It preserves the net cumulative-to-date balance (T1: every posting — invoices, credit notes, settling payments, write-offs, FX — to a trade-classified account is kept), uses a declared facet not a raw id (T2), and excludes the tax-receivable account and non-customer GJ (T3 — a GJ line inherits the classification of the account it posts to). No new engine derivation; classify_by_binding already exists.

D5 — Realization in two layers at their correct repair locations (do not conflate them):

(i) STORAGE — a bc-db forward migration (0039), repair location E, routed through the Database Change Protocol and operator-approved before apply. Add a column receivable_subtype_code (type text) to BOTH tenant.gl_account_classification_binding and contract.gl_account_classification_default: NULLABLE, NO default, with a CHECK set mirroring the existing account_class_code facet — CHECK (receivable_subtype_code IS NULL OR receivable_subtype_code IN ('trade','tax_statutory','other_non_trade')). It is nullable because the facet scopes only to receivable-type accounts (exactly as cash_flow_category_code is nullable); fail-closed (DEC-c46354 D4b) is enforced at RESOLUTION — a classify_by_binding field with output_facet=receivable_subtype that resolves against a NULL row REFUSES, never infers — NOT by a NOT NULL column and NOT by a default (a default would silently classify, violating fail-closed and Invariant VI). The nullable add is non-breaking for existing rows. No PostgreSQL enum: bc-db uses CHECK sets and lookup tables, never PG enums.

(ii) GRAMMAR / ENGINE — a bc-core change (repair B/D), SEPARATE from the migration, NOT a bc-db change. output_facet is a canonical-v2 classify_by_binding derivation PARAM VALUE, not a DB enum: add 'receivable_subtype' to the derivation's allowed output_facet values and the facet-to-column mapping (receivable_subtype -> receivable_subtype_code), and register the closed value set {trade, tax_statutory, other_non_trade} under the governed value-set (the D430 gate). The DB CHECK in (i) is the storage enforcement of the same closed set.

The DB Controller drafts (i) against this ADR (bc-db 0039) and proves it on an owner- and grant-faithful restore; apply is gated on the operator's formal DBCP sign-off, and the Architect Foundation-reviews the migration at its exact head before the DBCP presentation. No apply proceeds from this ADR.

D6 — Sequencing: this ADR (proposed) then operator DBCP plus the bc-db migration (DB Controller), then the receivable_subtype defaults plus the tenant/source-onboarding mapping for the demo/Kaveri source, then Metric binds the DSO numerator (D4, repair C), then the Chief brings LC5 up and re-proves the DSO figure to the cent (expect the ~0.35% non-trade removed — a downward shift that is correct, not a regression, per DEC-fa7c63 D-9(a)), then DSO certification unpauses under DEC-346ae7 as the (3b) robustness-harness / validated claim. The 261 GJ lines resolve IN or OUT by the classification of the account they hit, verified against LC5 at the re-prove.

## Status

Proposed. The Chief co-signs the design act; the operator gave GO on the path (2026-10-14, via the Chief); the DB apply remains gated on the operator's formal DBCP. This extends DEC-c46354 (its D6 new-facet path), realizes DEC-fa7c63 D-1 and D-9(a), and provides the trade-scoped numerator that DEC-346ae7's DSO cert-unpause depends on. DEC-c46354 and DEC-fa7c63 are not superseded.
