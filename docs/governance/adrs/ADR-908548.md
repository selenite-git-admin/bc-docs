# Track-C hold condition satisfied by D541 successor state — release Odoo SC/AC + source soft-references; ratify Odoo-first sequencing

## Context

Three holds cited "Track C" by name while the track itself was retired and replaced — leaving the holds pointing at a name with no live referent. The gate the holds actually intended (governed activation) is now substrate-enforced under D541, so keeping SC/AC + soft-refs frozen no longer protects anything: they are admission-boundary declarations of vendor semantics, need nothing from the certification lane, and deriving them waits on no world data (SDG-is-not-the-source doctrine). Releasing exactly SC + AC + soft-refs — and not OC/CC + metrics — keeps the certification-coupled legs held and avoids silently contradicting the recorded BC-first sequencing by ratifying the Odoo-first reality explicitly. The access boundary is recorded because the single licensed Enterprise admin user is in active use by the world-build arc; the authoring lane must not contend for it, and doctrinally must not treat any single instance as mapping authority anyway.

## Decision

**Determination.** "Track C" (the D519/D523 external-audit gate/wire track) no longer exists as a live program — it was retired by DEC-793e13 (D540, exchange retired) and superseded by DEC-c48b0f (D541, certification doctrine). The function the Track-C holds were waiting for — a real, enforced gate on metric activation — is delivered in the D541 successor form: `approved→active` closed by migration 46; certification gates activation and is substrate-enforced (lifecycle study F-036); D546 demotion took production actives 311→85 (54 certified + 31 residue); McfReadinessProjection (D548) is the live readiness surface. The operator determines the hold condition in DEC-58b56c (D524) and the out-of-scope clause of DEC-731c15 (D537) satisfied by this successor state.

**Released (amends the D537 out-of-scope line):** Source Contract (SC) and Admission Contract (AC) authoring for Odoo, plus D500 source soft-references. These are admission-boundary declarations of what Odoo-the-system emits — world-independent by doctrine, and advisory (soft-refs) by design.

**Still held:** OC/CC authoring and metric realization over Odoo ("chain §5"), pending certification-lane completion per PLN-457cd0 v12 §11.A (D532 consumer, certification wiring, rules pass, grandfathered-34 disposition) and TSK-fa743d. This preserves one-then-many and keeps the certification-coupled legs gated.

**Not lifted:** the independent Track-C-named holds on the mongo seed_metrics decommission (TSK-6fd293) and the db-bootstrap consolidation (TSK-8e49e0) — each remains its own operator call.

**Sequencing amendment to D524:** the recorded "BC first, Odoo second" ordering is amended to Odoo-first, ratifying the de facto program state (the Odoo 19 Enterprise pilot world-build arc, `pilot_ent`). The zero-MC-edit portability proof is unaffected — it needs the A/C legs whenever they land, in either order. D524's substance (BC + Odoo as the platform-proof pair; SAP ECC retained for the audit lane per D525) is otherwise unchanged; D524 and D537 remain decided — this is an amendment, not a supersession.

**Access boundary for the released lane:** SC/AC authoring takes its schema authority from Odoo 19 vendor source (the universal-mapping rule — mapping is per-system, never per-tenant or per-instance). The live `pilot_ent` Enterprise instance is OUT OF BOUNDS for this lane: it is the world-build arc's governed target, its RPC credential establishment is a gated r24 step of that arc, and sale/purchase/crm are not yet installed there. Optional live confirmation probes use a throwaway Odoo 19 Community container, never the licensed Enterprise instance. If an Enterprise-only field ever enters SC scope, instance evidence is gathered in a coordinated window after the world-build arc establishes credentials.
