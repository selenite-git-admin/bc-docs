# Source-system onboarding completeness: every tenant connection maps its company identity to the tenant's declared legal entities before it may feed evaluation

## Context

CONTEXT (verified read-only 2026-09-25, SES-bde699). D623/DEC-ea4523 removes the '*' legal-entity fallback, so every canonical row that needs a fiscal period must resolve a concrete legal entity through a governed source->legal-entity binding. Verified gaps: (1) there is no source-system onboarding service with mandatory steps or a completion state. Fragments exist: POST /connections (PlatformOnly; the controller calls createConnection(dto) without a tenant, so runtime.connection.tenant_id is never recorded; Kaveri's only connection kaveri-odoo-v3lc5 has tenant_id NULL; connection_status draft->connected is connectivity only); POST /schema-provisioner/onboard-connector (DEC-95687d/D369: chain walk, tenant/contract bindings, fact-table provisioning; its readiness means tables exist); reader flavor/binding authoring; POST /source-catalog/stacks (platform, tenant-agnostic). The tenant-onboarding SOP names an 'onboarding-incomplete' source state that nothing records. (2) Legal-entity and fiscal-calendar declarations have no governed writer (DEC-103acb/D504 Phase B parked, TSK-12d30b); Kaveri has zero legal entities and only a '*' calendar row. (3) The D623 binding substrate (bc-db tenant/0002, not yet applied anywhere) only supports a company key read from a row field and pins every mapping to SC and OC versions, so a source with no company concept (single-company CSV/sheet feed) or a request-scoped company (QuickBooks realm, Xero/Zoho organisation, Business Central company, Tally loaded company) cannot be mapped, and every contract re-version would force re-mapping. (4) Reader fetch lists are hand-kept per executor (odoo fields, sap-odata selectFields, sfdc fields) and are not checked against the contracts: Kaveri's reader never fetched company_id, so all 10,744 admitted journal-entry rows lack the company key. Operator doctrine (2026-09-25): a tenant and a legal entity are different concepts; tenant onboarding declares at least one legal entity; onboarding ANY source system requires mapping it to the tenant's legal entities (declare the entity first if missing); system onboarding is not complete without this and the other mandatory steps. Source company identifiers are only knowable at source onboarding, never at tenant onboarding, so the two acts must be separate and ordered.

## Decision

D1 - TENANT IS NOT A LEGAL ENTITY. A tenant is the BareCount customer account and data boundary (subscription, users, tenant database). A legal entity is a registered company whose books are observed. A tenant holds one or more legal entities; a legal entity belongs to exactly one tenant. Tenant onboarding MUST declare at least one legal entity with its fiscal calendar (reaffirms DEC-103acb/D504 D1). Legal-entity and calendar declarations carry NO source identifiers of any kind; they are the tenant's canonical identity and are writable at any time (acquisitions, discovered entities), not only at tenant onboarding.

D2 - A CONNECTION BELONGS TO EXACTLY ONE TENANT. A source connection (runtime.connection) is the tenant's instance of a source system. Its owning tenant must be recorded and immutable; a connection with no owning tenant cannot be onboarded.

D3 - MANDATORY, SOURCE-AGNOSTIC LEGAL-ENTITY MAPPING PER CONNECTION. Onboarding ANY source connection requires mapping its company identity onto the owning tenant's declared legal entities. Exactly two shapes, chosen by what the source exposes, never by vendor name: (a) VALUE-KEYED - the source exposes a company key (a record field such as a company code, or a company scope the reader observes and records per record); each observed key value maps to exactly one tenant legal entity (many values may map to one entity). (b) CONNECTION-SCOPED - the source has no company concept; the whole connection maps to one legal entity. Rule: use value-keyed whenever the source exposes a company key, even if it currently has one company, so a second company later is just another mapping. Where the key is read from is declared in the governed contracts (design plane), never hard-coded in the engine. If the target legal entity is not declared, it is declared first (D1), then mapped. Mappings are append-only and as-of (supersession = a later revision; retirement = a null-target revision; no back-dating past evaluated observations), per the D623 binding model. A mapping is a fact about the source instance: it is anchored on the connection (and the declared key domain), not on contract versions; the contract versions an evaluation used are recorded in its lineage (Invariant IV) instead.

D4 - SOURCE ONBOARDING COMPLETENESS. A connection is ONBOARDED only when every mandatory step passes: (1) owning tenant recorded; (2) connectivity check passed; (3) a reader configuration is attached; (4) the reader's fetch list covers every field the bound observation contracts declare (so a declared company key cannot silently go unread); (5) the fact tables for its bound contracts are provisioned (DEC-95687d/D369 readiness); (6) the legal-entity mapping is complete for the declared shape (connection-scoped: one mapping; value-keyed: every company key value known at onboarding mapped); (7) every mapped legal entity has a fiscal calendar in effect. A governed completeness read reports each step as pass/fail with an enumerated reason (Invariant VI) and triggers no evaluation. A company key value that appears after onboarding is refused at evaluation with an explicit no_legal_entity reason (D623 fail-closed, never a default entity) and reported as a completeness regression until mapped.

D5 - ENFORCEMENT PHASES. Phase 1: governed writers (legal entity + calendar per D504 D4; connection mapping) and the read-only completeness report. Phase 2: gate - observation for a connection that is not ONBOARDED is refused. Phase 3: bc-admin onboarding UI. Kaveri (odoo, value-keyed on its company key) is the first subject. Existing connections are brought to ONBOARDED through the same writers; there is no grandfathering by default.

FOUNDATION GATE. Repair location C (binding) plus the onboarding boundary; a design act (declaring tenant identity and source-to-entity mapping), not an execution-plane net. Invariant III - declarations and mappings append-only, corrections are new revisions. Invariant IV - every legal-entity reference is explicit; no '*' or blank identity; no vendor-name routing. Invariant VI - completeness emits enumerated reasons, never a silent pass. Portability test: a second source of a different shape onboards through D3 with no engine change.

NOT SOLVED HERE. Deriving reader fetch lists from observation contracts in general (TSK-b5ab8a class; D4 step 4 only checks coverage). Automatic discovery of a source's company list to prompt mapping. Per-legal-entity reporting-standard override. The D504 tenant-side activation gate (Phase C) remains its own track.

RELATIONSHIP. Source-side sibling of DEC-103acb/D504 (tenant side).
