# Metric validation is the three-part claim (meaning + formula asserted against the standard; calculation proven by the deterministic gate + a robustness harness); the AI panel is a flagger, not the correctness gate

## Context

The operator set the CORE CLAIM on 2026-10-13 ("very critical, always remembered") — the operational crystallization of the integrity-not-certification product thesis (2026-10-08): meaning and formula are commodity (knowable against the standard), and the real risk is calculation. The prior regime (DEC-92f709) over-weighted an inconsistent AI panel as a REQUIRED residual-FIT correctness verdict for meaning/formula that are commodity-knowable: on the sound DSO positive the panel upheld a false formula concern in 1 of 4 runs (5/5/5 the others). Requiring an AI panel to "verify FIT" of a known-standard meaning is ceremony re-discovering a known truth; the hard, novel thing to prove is that BareCount's mechanism CALCULATED the declared formula correctly, which deterministic + robustness-harness evidence establishes (DSO on LC5: reproduced 5/5 to the cent, deterministic). Foundation: Invariant VI (evidence is emitted, not inferred) — the three assertions are recorded evidence (the standard citation; the formula; the deterministic-gate + harness proof), not a reconstructed panel judgement. The integrity thesis: BareCount asserts truthful meaning/formula against the standard and PROVEN calculation — not a certification oracle. DEC-c48b0f §4 (anything machine-checkable belongs to a deterministic surface, never a panel) underpins (3) and the panel demotion. Four-gate check: (location) B — this declares the validation-regime contract semantics; (not an upper layer) this IS the product's top-level assertion, nothing sits above it; (not a lower layer) the panel-as-correctness-gate WAS lower-layer compensation — an inconsistent AI judge standing in for a knowable standard and for deterministic calculation proof — so demoting it to a flagger and asserting meaning/formula at the declaration against the standard is the correct layer; (design or execution) DESIGN act — it declares the regime (operator-set), which the Architect records. Supersedes nothing silently; amends DEC-92f709 as stated; coordinates with Metric (who asserts the per-metric meaning/formula in the contract) and the Chief as steward.

## Decision

BareCount's product claim for a metric — and therefore its validation regime — is the confident assertion of THREE parts: (1) MEANING is correct — the metric's definition matches the standard, commonly-understood meaning, asserted against a standard/authoritative reference (GAAP/IFRS/textbook/common definition) and recorded as that reference citation; (2) FORMULA is correct — the formula faithfully expresses that meaning, asserted and recorded; (3) CALCULATION is correct — BareCount's mechanism computes the declared formula correctly, robustly, reproducibly (METHOD integrity: given source data of the declared shape, computed correctly/robustly/reproducibly — not absolute correctness), proven by the DETERMINISTIC GATE (every machine-checkable class with a live deterministic check, DEC-c48b0f §4) PLUS a ROBUSTNESS HARNESS (LC5/Odoo: reproduces the figure deterministically on declared-shape source). The UNLOCK: BareCount did not invent the metrics or formulas — they are standard, public-domain, in LLM training knowledge — so (1) and (2) are COMMODITY: assert them confidently against the standard; do NOT build independent second-source ceremony to re-discover a known truth. The real, novel risk is (3) CALCULATION, which the deterministic gate + harness prove. The AI PANEL is DEMOTED to a FLAGGER of candidate (1)/(2) concerns — NEVER the correctness gate: a panel flag routes a meaning/formula convention question to Architect+operator (resolved against the standard), it does not reject a metric. A robustness-harness run is per-metric WHERE SCHEDULED, not a mandatory independent-two-source oracle for every metric; the certified-not-yet-validated set is NOT lesser (meaning/formula commodity-asserted; calculation harness-proven when scheduled; never rejected for lacking a harness run). This AMENDS DEC-92f709: its deterministic-gate-primary spine (item 1) is RETAINED and becomes part (3)'s calculation proof; its item (2) requirement that the panel return PANEL_VERIFIED on its residual scope is RE-SCOPED to the panel being an advisory flagger; cert-unpause is re-scoped to the 3-part claim (meaning+formula asserted vs standard; calculation proven by gate+harness), no longer gated on panel fitness. DEC-f21388 (no panel tuning) stands and is reinforced — the panel is not a gate to tune or declare fit.
