# Metric validation is the three-part claim (meaning + formula asserted against the standard; calculation proven by the deterministic gate + a robustness harness); the AI panel is a flagger, not the correctness gate

## Context

The operator set the CORE CLAIM on 2026-10-13 ("very critical, always remembered") — the operational crystallization of the integrity-not-certification product thesis (2026-10-08): meaning and formula are commodity (knowable against the standard), and the real risk is calculation. The prior regime (DEC-92f709) over-weighted an inconsistent AI panel as a REQUIRED residual-FIT correctness verdict for meaning/formula that are commodity-knowable: on the sound DSO positive the panel upheld a false formula concern in 1 of 4 runs (5/5/5 the others). Requiring an AI panel to "verify FIT" of a known-standard meaning is ceremony re-discovering a known truth; the hard, novel thing to prove is that BareCount's mechanism CALCULATED the declared formula correctly, which deterministic + robustness-harness evidence establishes (DSO on LC5: reproduced 5/5 to the cent, deterministic). Foundation: Invariant VI (evidence is emitted, not inferred) — the three assertions are recorded evidence (the standard citation; the formula; the deterministic-gate + harness proof), not a reconstructed panel judgement. The integrity thesis: BareCount asserts truthful meaning/formula against the standard and PROVEN calculation — not a certification oracle. DEC-c48b0f §4 (anything machine-checkable belongs to a deterministic surface, never a panel) underpins (3) and the panel demotion. Four-gate check: (location) B — this declares the validation-regime contract semantics; (not an upper layer) this IS the product's top-level assertion, nothing sits above it; (not a lower layer) the panel-as-correctness-gate WAS lower-layer compensation — an inconsistent AI judge standing in for a knowable standard and for deterministic calculation proof — so demoting it to a flagger and asserting meaning/formula at the declaration against the standard is the correct layer; (design or execution) DESIGN act — it declares the regime (operator-set), which the Architect records. Supersedes nothing silently; amends DEC-92f709 as stated; coordinates with Metric (who asserts the per-metric meaning/formula in the contract) and the Chief as steward.

## Decision

BareCount's product claim for a metric — and therefore its validation regime — is the confident assertion of THREE parts: (1) MEANING is correct — the metric's definition matches the standard, commonly-understood meaning, asserted against a standard/authoritative reference (GAAP/IFRS/textbook/common definition) and recorded as that reference citation; (2) FORMULA is correct — the formula faithfully expresses that meaning, asserted and recorded; (3) CALCULATION is correct — BareCount's mechanism computes the declared formula correctly, robustly, reproducibly (METHOD integrity: given source data of the declared shape, computed correctly/robustly/reproducibly — not absolute correctness). **Part (3) is proven in TWO tiers of EMITTED evidence, and a metric asserts only the tier it actually holds (Invariant VI — a certification claim never exceeds the evidence it carries):** **(3a) the DETERMINISTIC-GATE proof** — every machine-checkable class with a live deterministic check is green (DEC-c48b0f §4); this is COMPLETED at certification and proves method integrity for the mechanized classes; and **(3b) the ROBUSTNESS-HARNESS proof** — LC5/Odoo reproduces the figure deterministically on declared-shape source; the end-to-end reproduction, run per-metric WHERE SCHEDULED. The UNLOCK: BareCount did not invent the metrics or formulas — they are standard, public-domain, in LLM training knowledge — so (1) and (2) are COMMODITY: assert them confidently against the standard; do NOT build independent second-source ceremony to re-discover a known truth. The real, novel risk is (3) CALCULATION, which (3a) and (3b) prove. The AI PANEL is DEMOTED to a FLAGGER of candidate (1)/(2) concerns — NEVER the correctness gate: a panel flag routes a meaning/formula convention question to Architect+operator (resolved against the standard), it does not reject a metric.

**The two tiers bind two claim levels, so the asserted claim always matches the emitted evidence.** A **CERTIFIED** metric asserts (1)+(2) against the standard + **(3a)** deterministic-gate-proven calculation; it does **NOT** assert harness reproduction. A **VALIDATED** metric additionally carries **(3b)** — its scheduled harness run has reproduced the figure — which is the strongest calculation claim. The **certified-not-yet-validated** set is NOT lesser and is never rejected for lacking a harness run, but it carries **only** the gate-proven calculation claim (3a), never the harness-reproduced claim (3b), until its scheduled run completes. **No metric ever asserts (3b) without the completed harness evidence**, and a robustness-harness run is per-metric where scheduled, never a mandatory independent-two-source oracle for every metric.

This AMENDS DEC-92f709: its deterministic-gate-primary spine (item 1) is RETAINED and becomes part (3a); its item (2) requirement that the panel return PANEL_VERIFIED on its residual scope is RE-SCOPED to the panel being an advisory flagger; cert-unpause is re-scoped to the 3-part claim (meaning+formula asserted vs standard; calculation proven by (3a) the deterministic gate, with the (3b) harness/validated claim attaching per-metric where scheduled), no longer gated on panel fitness. DEC-f21388 is reconciled below (its no-tuning / single-capped-measurement discipline stands; its PASS→cert-unpause clause ceases).

### DEC-f21388 reconciliation (precise)

DEC-f21388 ("reduced-panel fitness is a single capped measurement — never a tuning loop") is reconciled against this regime, not wholly retained:

- **STANDS and is reinforced** — its discipline (items 1, 2, 4): the deterministic gate reduces the panel's scope by construction; any panel fitness exercise is ONE capped measurement of the current panel with no prompt/scope/mechanics change; a weak/fail result is a stop-and-report to the operator + Architect, never a tuning or re-measurement loop. The panel is not a gate to tune or declare fit.
- **CEASES to govern** — DEC-f21388 item (3) "A PASS proceeds to certification-unpause" and its closing sentence "the per-metric residual FIT verdict required by DEC-92f709 is unchanged." Under DEC-346ae7 the panel is an advisory flagger, cert-unpause is the 3-part claim (not panel fitness), and the panel's residual FIT verdict is no longer a required pass. Consequently a panel-fitness measurement that returns not-fit is **no longer a cert blocker** (it remains an honest data point and a stop-and-report, per the standing discipline). A matching Amendment 1 note is recorded on ADR-f21388.
