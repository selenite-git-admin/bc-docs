## The register

A ruling is a decision that belongs to the operator. This annex is the full register: the rulings already decided, the open rulings of the program as a whole, and the open questions raised by the dossier of each function. The main document carries the decided rulings and the open rulings needed first.

A **ruling** is a decision that belongs to the operator. Each is recorded here when made and is then written into the guide. Rulings are numbered in four series: F for the frame and the product; T for tenants, subscription and delivery; B for the engine, the contracts and proof; G for governance, access, operations and development. "Needed by" names the phase of the order of work that cannot finish without the ruling. Since ruling G39 the phases are numbered as the program text's "The order of work" gives them, and the "Needed by" columns below use that numbering. For a question of a function taken in part, the phase given is the spine phase when the question is about an act that ruling G39 names in that function's part, and the function's later phase otherwise.
